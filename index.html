Quick tips

  • Start every new pattern slowly. Half speed, get the motion right, then build up.
  • Your wrist does the work, not your arm. Let it swing like a pendulum.
  • Stay loose. If your hand feels tense, shake it out.
  • One pattern per week is plenty. Master the Basic first.
  • Try each pattern on a song you already know.
